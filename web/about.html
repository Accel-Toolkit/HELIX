About the project

Built for a real machine.

HELIX is developed at Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory for PIP-II — an 800-MeV superconducting-RF proton linac and the first US accelerator built with major international partners. Space-charge-dominated at low energy, PIP-II needs a tool that can match optics fast and then check the answer with high-fidelity 3-D space charge. That's the job HELIX was written for.
